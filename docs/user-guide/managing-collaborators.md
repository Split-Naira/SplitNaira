# User Guide: Managing Collaborators

## Overview
The SplitNaira dashboard allows project owners and administrators to seamlessly invite team members, assign role-based permissions, and manage access control for shared financial workspaces and split agreements.

---

## Inviting New Collaborators
1. Navigate to your project **Dashboard** and select the **Settings** tab.
2. Click on the **Collaborators** sub-menu.
3. Click the **[ + Invite Collaborator ]** button.
4. Enter the collaborator's registered email address and select their initial permission tier:
   * **Viewer**: Read-only access to transaction history and split statuses.
   * **Contributor**: Can propose and initiate new split agreements.
   * **Admin**: Full access to manage funds, settings, and collaborator roles.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

---

## Modifying Roles & Permissions
* Locate the collaborator in the active members list.
* Click the dropdown menu next to their role badge (`Viewer`, `Contributor`, `Admin`).
* Select the updated permission level and confirm the prompt to update access rights instantly.

---

## Removing Collaborators
* Find the user under the collaborators roster.
* Click the **Revoke Access** / **Remove** action button.
* Confirm the removal dialog. Revoking access immediately terminates their active session tokens and removes access to the workspace.
